ED Edition: The Wet-and-Dry Wipes System

Emergency-department nights are a different sport. Things are wet that shouldn't be, times are measured in codes and traumas, and your bag is the last clean surface you control. This edition splits everything into a dry bag and a wet bag.

Education only — this is a packing and hygiene-prep list, not medical, infection-control, or clinical advice. Decon and PPE decisions belong to your facility's policy and your infection-prevention team.

The rule

Nothing crosses from the wet bag into the dry bag without passing through wipes or a change of clothes. That's the whole system. Everything else is detail.

Smell-budget notes

  • Fragrance is a coworker courtesy issue. Keep it subtle or skip it; some patients and units are sensitive to strong scents.
  • A sealed change of clothes beats any amount of sprays-on-scrubs math.
  • If a shift beat you anyway, the win is just "the bag stayed halfway sane." Toss the tote clothes in laundry tonight; reset beats rumination.
