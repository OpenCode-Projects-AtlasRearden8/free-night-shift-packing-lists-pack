Packing-trap notes

  • The "I'll grab it in the morning" trap: the morning for a night shifter is a lie told by afternoon-you. Pack the night before you leave.
  • The water-bottle trap: an empty bottle in the bag is a bottle you never refill at 5 a.m. Fill it before you leave and again at first break.
  • The pen trap: three pens minimum. You will lend exactly two.
  • The pantry-down-the-hall trap: meds-surg nights do not wander to vending machines. Assume every snack must already be in the bag.
