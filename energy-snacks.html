The four snack archetypes

The Anchor

Does the heavy lifting at break.

  • Burrito-style wrap, foil-packed
  • Couscous or ramen-jar meals you just add water to
  • Soup in a wide-mouth thermos

The Pick-Me-Up

Small, protein-forward, no sugar crash.

  • Trail mix with some chocolate, not all
  • Hummus cup + pretzels (keep chilled per label)
  • Cheese stick + clementine

The Scrub-Pocket Survivor

Endures 12 hours at body temperature.

  • Jerky or biltong
  • Roasted edamame or chickpeas
  • Granola/protein bar that isn't mostly straight sugar
  • Peanut-butter crackers

The Unsung Hero

Boring until hour 9, then heroic.

  • Plain water from your own bottle
  • Gum or mints
  • One piece of fruit, promised to yourself

Not-coffee caffeine and alertness notes (educational)

  • If you're avoiding caffeine tonight for sleep-quality reasons, common swaps people use include cold water, a short walk, bright light at the right time, movement, and spacing snacks — see the fatigue one-pager for the light-and-nap part.
  • Caffeine timing conventions vary a lot between people; if you use caffeine, plan it with your own sleep schedule in mind and ask your clinician if you have questions.
  • Energy drinks vary widely; read labels, know your own limits and any care-team guidance that applies to you.
