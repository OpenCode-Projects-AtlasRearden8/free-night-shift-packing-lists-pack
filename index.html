Night-Shift Packing Lists Pack

Tear-out packing checklists and locker helpers for night-shift nurses, late-shift workers, float/agency staff, and anyone who leaves the house at 6:15 p.m. with 15 minutes to pack. Print a page, cut along the dashed lines, tape it inside your bag or locker door, and stop reinventing the same mental list every shift.

Education only, never medical advice. These pages are packing lists and general wellness suggestions from one human to another. Nothing here is medical, clinical, or dietary advice — talk to your own clinician about anything health-related, including sleep, fatigue, caffeine, and food choices.

What's in the pack

  • 12-Hour Med-Surg Checklist The classic full-bag tear-out plus a pocket card
  • Float / Agency Bag Checklist For unknown units, borrowed lockers, and first-nights
  • ED Wet-and-Dry Wipes Edition Two-bag system for the messiest shifts
  • Weather Add-On Sheets Deep winter, summer heat, and rain-commute insert cards
  • Coffee-Free Energy Snack Ideas Foods sorted by shift timing, with packability notes
  • Locker-Stash Inventory Card Fill-in card so you stop rebuying sock four-packs
  • Fatigue-Prep One-Pager Sleep setup, light, naps, and the too-tired-to-drive plan

How to use it

  1. Open the page that matches your scenario.
  2. On screen, tick boxes as you pack — your progress saves in your browser only (local storage, no accounts, no server). Use Uncheck all to reset it.
  3. To print: use your browser's Print button or the page's Print button. The pages are laid out for US Letter with 0.5-inch margins and cut along the dashed lines to tear out the cards.

How this pack is different

  • Not a swap pack. This is about packing your bag. Trading and covering shifts is a separate topic — see the free-nurse-shift-swap-pack.
  • Not a manual. A full night-shift survival guide is a different, longer thing. These are one-page tear-outs meant for a printer, not a reader.
  • Zero backend. Plain HTML files. No accounts, no tracking, no data leaves your device. Checkbox progress lives in your own browser's local storage.
